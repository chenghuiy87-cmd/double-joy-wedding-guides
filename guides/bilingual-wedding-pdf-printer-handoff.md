# Before Sending Bilingual Wedding PDFs to a Printer

A free handoff template for couples who have finished the wording and exported a Chinese-English card set. A successful PDF export does not establish how a physical print will look. Ask the printer to confirm their requirements before paying for the whole run.

## Check the actual file first

Open the exported PDF. Compare the year, month, day and time with your approved event notes on **every page**. Check the Chinese and English names, missing characters, line breaks and RSVP information. If the tea ceremony and reception are separate events, confirm which event each card describes. Correct the editable source and export a new PDF after any change.

A 5 × 7-inch PDF page measures 360 × 504 PDF points. That establishes the document size; it does not establish paper choice, bleed, physical trim, colour or print quality. Keep the editable source draft separately. The printable PDF cannot restore editable fields.

## Copy this blank request

Fill this in privately with your own details. Do not post your guest list or contact information in a public repository.

> Hello, I would like a quote for printing the attached wedding stationery PDF. Please confirm the points below before production.
>
> - Finished card size: ______
> - Number of pages and copies of each page: ______
> - Single-sided or double-sided: ______
> - Paper preference, or the options I would like quoted: ______
> - Date I need the finished cards: ______
> - Delivery or collection preference: ______
>
> Do you require bleed, crop marks or a different PDF specification? Please tell me if this file does not meet your requirements. Do not change the wording or scale the pages without my approval.
>
> Please confirm that the Chinese characters and line breaks render correctly. What is the cost and process for a physical proof before the full order? I would like to approve that proof and the final quote before production.
